welcome to jot
A quiet place to write markdown. This document is a short tour, and it's yours: edit it, keep it, or delete it. You can always get it back from the ? at the bottom of the sidebar.
writing
Everything is saved as you type. There's no save button, and Cmd/Ctrl + S does nothing on purpose.1
• Lists continue when you press Enter.1. Numbered items count up by themselves.2. Enter on an empty item ends the list.
Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + I tidies the whole document: bullets, emphasis and table columns.2 One undo reverses it.
seeing it rendered
Press Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + V to open the rendered view beside this one. It scrolls along with the editor.
