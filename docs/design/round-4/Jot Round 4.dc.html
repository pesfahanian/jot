jot / round 4 — the app's shape
Shell, settings, palette, annotations, ai mode, motion
Eighteen frames, built from v3 tokens and the floating-panel shell, with the last round's diff, Farsi and PDF frames as the reference. Light and dark share every shape; only colour changes. Decisions the brief left open, and pushback, come first.
Where each control went
Sidebar header: jot · new document · search · sort and filter (sort and colour filter merged into one menu) · hide sidebar. Sidebar foot: settings ⌘, · import… · ?. Pane: tab strip + now opens a small menu (document, comparison); split, render, export ▾ and ⋯ stay. Status bar: ai mode replaces review style on the left; dir, cursor, counts, a new notes count and the minimap toggle on the right.
Dropped in favour of the palette
New comparison leaves the sidebar (tab strip + and ⌥⌘N). The theme icon leaves the sidebar foot (settings, palette, ⇧⌘L): it's set once and rarely touched. The AI Provider item goes into settings. Search stays visible because it's how people find documents; sort and filter stay but share one button.
Collapsed sidebar
The sidebar leaves entirely. Its toggle moves to the start of the first pane's tab strip, with the wordmark beside it, so the app is still named. ⌘B in both states. Double-click on empty tab strip or below the list makes a new document; the list shows a faint hint only when it has room.
Import
Sidebar foot, next to settings, because it fills the list. Also in settings → storage, the palette (⌘O) and by dropping files on the list. Its counterpart, export → whole workspace, stays in export ▾.
Palette shortcut
⌘K. VS Code's ⇧⌘P opens a private window in Firefox and can't be taken there; ⌘K is free in Chrome, Safari and Firefox pages. ⇧⌘P is bound as an alias where the browser allows it. Documents and commands share one list with no prefix; ↵ opens here, ⌘↵ beside.
Highlight colours
Three, from tag slots green, clay and indigo, mixed onto document (light 22/18/18%, dark 30%). Not yellow: --mark yellow already means selection and search, and a highlight that looks like a selection is a bug report. The right-click row shows all three; the row itself applies the last used.
Underline
1.5px solid secondary ink, 3px offset. Straight and colourless, so it can't be read as a spelling or grammar mark (those are coloured and wavy).
How notes are read
A dotted underline on the text and a 7px square on the gutter rule. Click either for a popover under the text: read, edit in place, delete. Not a margin: panes go down to 260px. "notes N" in the status bar lists every note in document order.
What ai mode does to the shell
The sidebar hides. Other panes are parked, not closed, and come back exactly as they were. The chat takes the sidebar's slot at 360px; the document fills the rest and stays editable. The status bar stays; its left cell becomes "ai mode · esc to leave" with provider and model beside it. A full translation opens one second pane for the draft.
AI notes vs personal notes
Shape, not colour, so it survives greyscale and colour blindness. AI note: a numbered inverted pill and a 1px outline around the text. It's an instruction, numbered because the queue and the chat refer to it. Personal note: a dotted underline and a gutter square, no number. AI notes never print or export.
The results switch
A results bar under the tab strip: review | diff as a segmented control, selected in strong neutral. In diff, a second segmented control "as decided / if all accepted" with counts, outlined in tertiary ink so it reads as a control, and the right column header says which one you're looking at.
Chat proposals have no kind
Only the style review sorts findings into quick fix, check fix and your call. Chat and note proposals are drawn in neutral ink with "from ai note 1" or "from chat", so colour in the results always means style review.
New tokens and components
--hl-1…3, three motion durations, two easings. Components: modal (settings), segmented control, switch, key chip, ai note pill, chat message, notes list. No new weight, radius or font.
Push back: browser-owned keys
⌘N, ⌘T and ⌘W belong to the browser, so "new document" can't be ⌘N in Chrome. I used ⌥N and ⌥⌘N, and the shortcut editor flags browser keys inline instead of letting you bind them and fail silently.
Push back: review kind colours
Teal, amber and violet aren't in v3; its review tiers are deliberately hueless (§1.6). I mapped them to tag teal (also diff "added"), punctuation amber (also a proofing underline) and tag plum (v3 dropped violet on dark). That needs a ruling before build.
Push back: one word for notes
The brief says "comments" in the PDF and "notes" elsewhere. I used notes everywhere; the card row reads "off / highlights / + notes" to fit 320px.
Push back: translation without headings
"Section by section" needs sections. Documents with no headings should fall back to batches of paragraphs, or a long note becomes one slow request.
Push back: auto-fix and Farsi
Auto-fix spelling should be off on right-to-left lines until it's tested there, and each fix should be its own undo step.

Never animates
Typing, the cursor, scrolling, tab and document switching, theme changes, sorting and filtering the list, every counter, line numbers, accept and reject (state changes land at once), toasts except their countdown line, and anything inside a document.
Reduced motion
If the system asks for reduced motion, or settings → appearance → animations is off, every duration is 0. The only exception is the auto-fix flash, which becomes a static 1 s wash. The settings switch shows as off and locked, with "your system asks for reduced motion" beneath it.
No new easing elsewhere
Two curves, three durations. No springs, no overshoot, no stagger. If something needs a fourth duration, it probably shouldn't move.
